# Graduated hints

Try the problem before opening each hint. Stop when you can continue independently.

## Hint 1

Hint 1: Socho ki agar tum ek nayi character append kar rahe ho, toh tumhe past string ke baare me strictly kya-kya yaad rakhne ki zaroorat hai?

## Hint 2

Hint 2: Sirf 2 values matter karti hain: total count of 'A' (jo 0 ya 1 ho sakti hai) aur consecutive 'L' at the end (jo 0, 1, ya 2 ho sakti hai).

## Hint 3

Hint 3: 6 states banao: dp[a][l] jahan a in {0, 1} aur l in {0, 1, 2}. Transition equations likho har character ('P', 'A', 'L') ke addition ke liye.
