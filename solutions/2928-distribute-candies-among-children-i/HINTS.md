# Graduated hints

Try the problem before opening each hint. Stop when you can continue independently.

## Hint 1

Total children fixed hain: exactly 3 children.

## Hint 2

Agar pehle do children ki candies fix kar lein (say i aur j), to teesre child ki candies directly n - i - j ho jayengi.

## Hint 3

Bas check karo ki kya teesre child ki value valid range [0, limit] mein aati hai.
