# Graduated hints

Try the problem before opening each hint. Stop when you can continue independently.

## Hint 1

Notice karo ki answer me sirf 3-digit even numbers hi ho sakte hain. Aise kitne possible numbers exist karte hain overall?

## Hint 2

100 se 999 ke beech me sirf 450 even numbers hain! Kya hum pure digits array ke permutations nikalne ki jagah in 450 numbers ko check kar sakte hain?

## Hint 3

Har candidate number ke digits ki frequency compare karo input array ke digits frequency ke saath. Agar candidate ke paas required digits sufficient hain, to wo valid hai.
